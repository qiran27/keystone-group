extend plaintext-marker checks beyond the initially registered regions to the
Host process's heap, stack, anonymous and shared mappings, live temporary
buffers, and referenced pages on the exercised kernel/file I/O path. Markers
are generated in enclave-private EPM; the negative Host collector does not
construct or search for the complete marker. Content is matched offline by an
independent verifier. Object/mapping inventories, successful-read ledgers,
physical-page aliases, lifetimes and missed phases are retained separately.
EPM and protected TSR ranges are excluded with physical-range metadata and
their isolation is assessed by the independent access tests. Separate positive
runs must detect every intentionally planted regional control before a negative
run can support an expanded-scope conclusion. [All new counts and log references

ASSUMED RESULTS, PENDING EXPERIMENTAL VALIDATION.
If all mandatory regions and regional controls pass, the conclusion is limited
to no observed test-plaintext marker in the covered regions and sampling times
of the tested configurations. Missing/unreadable objects, failed workloads,
enabled but unexamined swap/dump paths and missed mandatory phases are reported
as coverage incomplete, not as zero-hit passes. The observation module is test
instrumentation and does not change the assumption of an untrusted production
kernel. Its dumps cannot establish a trustworthy negative conclusion when the
tested kernel is allowed to fabricate evidence. The experiment neither proves
continuous absence of leakage nor replaces side-channel or rollback analysis.
